## Theme / area

Smart tutoring (Monitoria inteligente) — campus life and community

## Problem

Our application aims to address the difficulties students may face in locating and accessing tutoring sessions tailored to their academic needs. 
The application is primarily designed for students who require study support and wish to find assistance from tutors for specific subjects.

## Solution

Monitoria Inteligente is an application developed to facilitate connections between students and tutors, allowing students to find available tutoring sessions based on their specific needs and subjects. The platform aims to make the academic support process more organized and accessible, simplifying how information related to tutoring sessions is accessed and managed.
